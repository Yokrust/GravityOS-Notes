# River as the resumable stream transport for Gravity

Gravity will use River as the transport layer for live, typed, resumable agent streams, but Gravity will keep ownership of `Run`, `Thread`, `Trace`, and `Agent Activity Stream` semantics. We chose this because Gravity needs a clean way to expose, consume, and resume stream delivery across interruptions without hand-rolling chunk framing, resume tokens, and lifecycle handling, while still preserving Gravity-owned meaning and persistence in the application layer. Gravity will integrate River through a custom Electron IPC bridge rather than adopting a TanStack Start or SvelteKit adapter, since the product is an Electron app and the stream boundary belongs in Gravity's runtime/infrastructure layers, not in a web framework.
